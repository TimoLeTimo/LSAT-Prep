# Official LSAT Practice Test Research Directory

**Checked:** November 24, 2026  
**Purpose:** Locate a large amount of official LSAT practice material for personal study and high-level product research. This is a resource directory, not permission to copy content into the LSAT Prep app.

## 1. Best starting point: LawHub

- [LSAC — Official LSAT Practice Tests](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/prepare/official-lsat-practice-tests)
- [LawHub](https://lawhub.org/)
- [LSAC — Free Official LSAT Prep and LawHub Advantage](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/prep)

LSAC's official practice-test page currently states that four full four-section Official LSAT PrepTests are available free through LawHub. It also describes LawHub Advantage as providing one year of access to an extensive library of full Official LSAT PrepTests. The catalog is account-based and can change, so the live LawHub account is the authoritative place to see exactly which tests are available to a subscriber.

**Practical plan:** Start with the four free full tests. If a large official test library is useful to the student, compare the current LawHub Advantage price and terms on LSAC/LawHub before purchasing. Do not hard-code a test count or price into the app.

## 2. Publicly disclosed tests

- [LSAC — LSAT Disclosed Tests](https://www.lsac.org/lsat-disclosed-tests)

LSAC's page lists disclosure booklets for the 2023–2024, 2024–2025, and 2025–2026 testing cycles. Each contains three scored sections from the test administration date identified in the booklet. LSAC explicitly distinguishes these disclosed tests from Official LSAT PrepTests.

These are valuable for seeing publicly disclosed examples and the current broad section mix. They are still LSAC intellectual property and should not be copied into the app without written permission.

## 3. Official prep books

- [LSAC — LSAT Prep Books](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/lsat-prep/prep-books)
- [The Official LSAT SuperPrep](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/lsat-prep/prep-books-ebooks/official-lsat-superprepr)

The official catalog lists:
- The Official LSAT SuperPrep
- The Official LSAT SuperPrep II
- The New Official LSAT TriplePrep Volumes 2 through 20

The catalog's TriplePrep Volume 2 page identifies PrepTests 155–157. LSAC describes the TriplePrep series as reflecting the LSAT design beginning with the August 2024 administration, while older SuperPrep books are also described as historical resources. Check the specific edition and contents carefully: older LSAT material may include the former Analytical Reasoning/Logic Games section and should not be treated as current-format practice without checking.

## 4. Official licensed content providers

- [LSAC — Official LSAT Prep overview and content licensees](https://www.lsac.org/lsat/prep)

LSAC maintains a list of companies that license official LSAT content. This may be useful if the student wants additional official questions in a third-party product. A subscription or purchase from a licensee does not automatically grant permission to extract questions into this private app.

## 5. Rights and policy references

- [LSAC — Terms and Conditions of Use](https://www.lsac.org/about/lsac-policies/terms-conditions-use)
- [LSAC — Candidate Agreement, 2026–2027](https://www.lsac.org/about/lsac-policies/lsac-candidate-agreement/2026-2027)

LSAC's website terms describe the site's content as protected intellectual property and restrict copying, storing, reproducing, distributing, and creating derivative works without prior written consent. The Candidate Agreement addresses secure test material and states that publicly released practice/sample tests may not be copied, duplicated, or used in other works without prior written approval.

This directory was checked on November 24, 2026. Revisit the linked official pages before relying on counts, pricing, test structure, or terms.
